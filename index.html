How does Cash on Delivery work?

A PKR 350 advance is sent via JazzCash/EasyPaisa before dispatch. The balance is paid when your order arrives.

How do I track my order?

Open “My Orders” at the top. It shows the status of every order placed from this device. You can also message us on WhatsApp with your order ID.

Can I get something engraved?

Yes. Products under “Made Just For You” take your name or message (up to 20 characters) at checkout.
